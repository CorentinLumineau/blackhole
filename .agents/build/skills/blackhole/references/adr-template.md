# ADR Authoring Template

Required skeleton for Design Track staged ADRs (`planner.md` §4.8). Every section below must
appear as a `##` heading in the staged body — a missing section blocks the plan (`status: blocked`).

## Status

One line: bare enum token (`accepted`, `superseded`, or `deprecated`) or evidence-bearing prose
whose leading token agrees with frontmatter (see `doc-governance.md` § ADR Status Enum).

## Context

What problem or constraint forced this decision? Link prior ADRs or audits where relevant.

## Decision

The chosen approach — imperative, one primary option. Cross-cutting constraints promoted to
`ARCHITECTURE.md` cite `(ADR-{NNN})` per `planner.md` §4.8 Trigger A.

## Alternatives Considered

At least one rejected option with a one-line why-not per row (trade-off matrix rows may be
referenced by name).

## Consequences

Positive and negative outcomes — operational, maintenance, and protocol-surface effects.
